# OneMuslim — Landing Experience Blueprint

## Product direction
OneMuslim is a welcoming Muslim social, learning, streaming, community, and marketplace platform. The landing experience should feel like a premium, modern social feed rather than a long documentation page.

## Landing principles
- Inclusive to people discovering Islam, new Muslims, returning Muslims, and Muslims seeking deeper study.
- Short, scannable sections with horizontal carousels for related content.
- X/Twitter-inspired feed rhythm: compact posts, strong hierarchy, sticky header, right-rail discovery, mobile bottom navigation.
- Dark-first, elegant, high-tech, Muslim-inspired without excessive decorative clichés.
- Do not overwhelm the first screen.
- Keep core lessons protected; community content is additive.

## Learning paths
### Easy Path — recommended
Gentle first route through Allah, Qur'an, forgiveness, prayer, and daily life.
- Short lessons.
- One question at a time.
- Verse-based answers.
- Practical first steps.

### Advanced Path
For users ready for Qur'an, Tafsir, Hadith, Seerah, history, theology, comparative religion, and apologetics.
- Structured checklist.
- Timelines.
- Quizzes and scoring.
- Sources and further reading.
- Users may skip lessons, but can branch back when a later lesson needs a skipped foundation.

## Required learning content
Foundations include Allah, Shahadah, Tawhid, Qur'an, Mus'haf, Tafsir, Hadith, Sunnah, Seerah, Dua, Salah, Wudu, Tawbah, Jesus/Isa, Qiblah, Shirk, Iman, Sawm, Zakah, and Ummah.

## Prayer Academy
Teach salah visually: standing/qiyam, bowing, prostration/sujud, and sitting. Include what to say and short quizzes.

## Explore
Include concise entry points for:
- Why One God / Tawhid
- Jesus in Islam
- History and Christianity
- Prophethood
- Qur'an and revelation
- Isaiah 42
- Qur'anic reading and tafsir

Historical claims should be presented with reputable scholarship and primary-source context. Avoid oversimplified claims such as reducing complex early-Christian history to a single event.

## Shahadah
Explain the testimony in emotionally understandable terms. Use the analogy that saying “I love my mother” does not create love; it affirms what one already knows and believes. Present the Shahadah as an affirmation after understanding and believing.

## Starter pack
Qur'an translation/Arabic edition, prayer mat, modest clothing, and hijab for sisters who choose it.

## Community
Invite reflections, encouragement, and questions. Keep foundational lessons stable while allowing community contributions around them.

## Design rule
Prefer a compact feed + carousels over a very long static landing page. Related content belongs in horizontal scrolling groups rather than separate full-width sections.
