# 1Muslim Avatar Bots A/B — Shared Production Brief

Status: approved design brief; generation agents must be explicitly connected/restarted before images are produced. No production avatar data or assets changed by this document.

## Roles and handoff
- Bot A: male faceless avatars. Bot B: female faceless avatars.
- Work in alternating pairs: A makes the male version of a theme, then B makes the corresponding female version. Review alignment before advancing to the next theme.
- First collection order: Astronaut, Doctor, Scientist, Engineer, Pilot, Architect, Robotics Specialist, Ocean Explorer.
- Each bot must use the same framing template and save a matching 1:1 image; no labels, watermarks, lettering, or faces.
- Female designs retain modest hijab styling where compatible with the theme. Male designs may use a kufi where compatible. Both remain faceless metallic figures.

## Locked shared framing
- Square canvas (1:1), pure black background, no environmental scene or circular ring baked into the asset.
- Straight-on centered bust portrait, eye-level camera, shoulders symmetrical, no tilt.
- Smooth featureless reflective metallic faceplate; no eyes, nose, mouth, skin, or human facial details.
- Top of head/helmet at 12% of canvas height (allow 10–14%); centerline at x=50% (±2%).
- Chin/faceplate lower edge around y=63–67%; shoulders begin around y=72–78%; crop bust at bottom.
- Subject silhouette occupies ~78–84% of canvas width at its broadest point; preserve 8–11% side breathing room, avoid shoulder clipping when possible.
- Match scale, camera focal length, lighting direction, neck length, faceplate position and overall silhouette between A and B.
- Reference: supplied silver metallic male and female portraits. Silver is a mandatory universal material/color requirement for every theme, not merely a quality reference.
- ALL themes MUST remain predominantly polished silver/chrome from head to clothing and accessories. Never recolor the base character or outfit into theme colors.\n- Differentiate themes primarily by sculptural outfit, props, seams, and material finish; use only very faint, tasteful colored reflected rim lighting (e.g. blue for astronaut, cool teal for doctor), never saturated paint, neon glow, or tinted metal.
- No text, badges, logos or ornamental UI borders embedded in the image; website applies its own frame.

## Theme-specific details
- Astronaut: futuristic sealed collar, brushed titanium/ceramic, faint cool blue reflected light.
- Doctor: refined clinical coat or futuristic medical uniform, faint cool teal reflected light, no exposed face.
- Scientist: laboratory coat, subtle glass/metal instrument motifs, faint violet reflected light.
- Engineer: precision technical jacket and geometric construction accents, subtle warm silver reflections.
- Pilot: streamlined aviation uniform and polished helmet elements, faint sapphire reflections.
- Architect: sculptural structured clothing, silver with understated warm reflections.
- Robotics Specialist: carbon-fiber and chromed articulated panels, subtle emerald reflected light.
- Ocean Explorer: advanced diving collar, pearl-silver metal with faint blue reflections, subtle pressure-seal details.

## Acceptance checklist (before saving each pair)
1. Both images 1:1 with identical centerline and head height (within 2%).
2. At least 10% clear space above the head; adequate side margins.
3. Faceless, modest, predominantly silver/chrome throughout; faint lighting only, never theme-colored outfits.
4. Matching camera angle, crop and lighting; only outfit/theme/gender presentation changes.
5. Test rapid crossfade between paired avatars and across themes; reject visible jumps.
6. Deliver individual files named avatar-<theme>-male.png and avatar-<theme>-female.png, with a manifest containing dimensions and framing coordinates.

Do not overwrite existing avatars or deploy assets without review.
